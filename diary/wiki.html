Example article (wiki)

A reusable article layout built from the same visual system as the 2026 diary, adapted for long-form reference pages.

Example Article is a demonstration of how the diary design can become a broader wiki-like template without losing the visual character of lewis.lc.

The main column is deliberately simple and editorial. It can hold biography, episode information, places, projects, memories, websites, people or anything else that benefits from a reference-page structure.

Background

This area behaves like a conventional article rather than a table. The typography, spacing, borders and muted colours follow the same restrained language as the diary while giving the text more room to breathe.

article image
Optional image caption, source note or contextual description.

Details

Sections can be as long or short as necessary. Important phrases can link to other pages in the archive, allowing the site to gradually grow into an interconnected personal encyclopaedia.

“The point is to feel like lewis.lc first, and a wiki second.”

A smaller subsection

Smaller headings are useful where an article needs more structure without turning every paragraph into a major section.

archive note

This kind of box can be used for citations, production notes, trivia, corrections or personal commentary.

Legacy

For content-heavy subjects, the tabs prevent the page becoming an endless wall of material: narrative text stays in Overview, chronology moves to History, images go in Gallery and scraps of information can live in Notes.
