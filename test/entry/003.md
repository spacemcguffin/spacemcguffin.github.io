Title: tonight, i am alone
Date: 2026-12-01
Time: 8:50pm

---

*3:50pm*. Woke up this morning to some new RTD quotes, in which he basically comes across as bitter and miserable that his new era of *Doctor Who* crashed and burned.

He says his era should be more appreciated, and that at least he appreciates it. He reckons the Billie Piper cliffhanger is a ‘piece of piss’ to get out of. He says that the version of *The Reality War* which ended mid-regeneration on a fade to black felt like the ‘death of the show.’ Funny that, because everyone instantly saw that ending and felt like it was the most creatively bankrupt, desperate last minute addition ever. Ncuti glowing and fading out may have been more solemn, but at least it would’ve had a certain dignity to it.

RTD also told us that the Christmas special this year would’ve featured an old English village, intercut with a mysterious spaceship. There would’ve been an outer-space festival called Winternox and lots of Daleks, ‘with something special hidden away.’ Then there’s a confused mess of information, about it being pushed back to Easter 2027, production woes, and RTD himself going to BBC execs to tell them they should probably not bother about making it (even though it was officially commissioned). Bizarre. But oh well. I know I wasn’t looking forward to it, and I suspect it would’ve been just another stain on the show. *The Reality War* ended the RTD2 era in a messy way, but at least it put us out of our misery quickly, rather than us having to wait another 18 months for one measly shitty Christmas special.

Anyway. RTD is old news now. Because no sooner did these quotes appear online, the BBC issued a press release about the upcoming CBeebies version of the show. *TARDIS Crew* will feature a brand new youngish Doctor, along with his companions – Hannah, Beastie and Toast-IE (who is an actual toaster robot, and who would also make a cute toy). There’s a promo picture too, featuring the main characters, the TARDIS, and some adorable looking Daleks. The show is set to air late 2027 or early 2028, with 52 episodes coming in at 11 minutes each.

It might be a show aimed squarely at preschool kids, but I’m excited to see it. I also realised earlier that it’s the first official *Doctor Who* thing since 2004 not to have RTD, Moffat or Chibnall attached to it in some way. It looks fun, it feels fresh. It seems to be completely separate to the main show, which is a good thing, and I’m optimistic that it’ll do well with the audience the BBC are aiming to capture with it. Over to you, Blue Zoo.
