First appearance Pilot Episode

Edge End High School is the main school in South Park. It is one of the oldest known locations on the show and has appeared in multiple incarnations.

Appearance

Edge End High School is a predominantly single-storey school, but with staffrooms and a library area on an upper floor.

IN RUINS

The first appearance of Edge End is in the pilot episode, where it has been destroyed and delapidated for multiple years. It remains in this state for the majority of Season 1.

REBUILT

During Season 2, the school is rebuilt, and has two predominant chimneys as a focal feature. To the rear is a giant playing field where the kids hang out during break times.
