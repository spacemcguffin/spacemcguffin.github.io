List of South Park episodes

South Park is an American animated television series created by Trey Parker and Matt Stone. This page presents the first five seasons in an encyclopaedia-style format with sample episode entries.

The series premiered on Comedy Central on 13 August 1997. Browse the five-season overview or jump to an individual season to explore the episode tables.
