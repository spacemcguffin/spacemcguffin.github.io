Lewis is a British personal website creator and diary writer whose online projects explore nostalgia, early social networking and personal web publishing. His website, lewis.lc, serves as a place to experiment with HTML and CSS and maintain a catalogue of diary entries.

Having used the internet for more than two decades, Lewis describes a gradual retreat from public social media towards a quieter, more private online presence. His profile pages combine reflections on that transition with an affection for the web culture of the 2000s.

Background

Lewis describes himself as an introvert, minimalist and night owl. On his personal profile, he reflects on spending more than twenty years online and becoming increasingly selective about his use of social media. His faux MySpace page presents a deliberately nostalgic, playful version of an earlier internet identity, under the display name Lewishh :].

The MySpace-inspired profile lists his location as the United Kingdom and his age as 33. Its humorous details, including an occupation of “Time traveller”, are part of the page's retro presentation.

Online history

Early platforms and social media

Lewis recalls using a wide range of social platforms and publishing services, including Piczo, Bebo, Blogger, MySpace, Dailybooth, Tumblr, LiveJournal, WordPress, Facebook and Twitter, as well as online forums. He describes the earlier internet as something he misses, while also noting that its pace and culture have changed.

After years of frequent social media activity, including more than 25,000 tweets, he deleted much of his earlier public online presence during the mid-2010s. He now primarily keeps accounts for browsing and private communication.

Diary writing

Between 2008 and 2012, Lewis kept an online diary on LiveJournal. Much of the original archive was subsequently lost, although he recovered many of the 2008 and 2009 entry titles using the Wayback Machine. He resumed diary writing in 2021, keeping the newer entries offline and private.

The diary archive is described on his About page as the one enduring feature of his personal website.

Personal website and web design

Lewis uses lewis.lc to experiment with HTML, CSS and page layouts. Many of the projects are temporary or exploratory rather than permanent publications. His MySpace-inspired page recreates the visual language of an older social network, complete with interests, blurbs, a friend space, blog previews and period-style comments.

Interests

Music

Music is a recurring interest across both profiles. Artists listed include Adam and the Ants, Alestorm, Alex Day, Alex Warren, Alphaville, Chameleon Circuit, Evanescence, Green Day, Henry Moodie, HIM, Linkin Park, My Chemical Romance, Nightwish, Panic! at the Disco, Pet Shop Boys, Rammstein, Sam Fender, The Killers, Tom Grennan and Ville Valo, among many others.

The faux MySpace profile features “Heartache Every Moment” by HIM, while the About page also lists HIM as the artist being listened to at its last stated update in October 2026.

Film and television

Television interests listed on the MySpace page include Doctor Who, South Park, The Simpsons (particularly seasons 1–10), Taskmaster, EastEnders, Squid Game, Big Brother (series 3–7), Courage the Cowardly Dog, The Powerpuff Girls, Dexter's Laboratory, Knightmare and Eurovision.

Favourite films listed there include Back to the Future, Psycho, Saw, Batman (1989), Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, You Only Live Twice and On Her Majesty's Secret Service.

Games and other interests

Games named on the MySpace page include Minecraft, RollerCoaster Tycoon, Planet Coaster, Theme Hospital, Croc, The Simpsons: Hit & Run and Grand Theft Auto: Vice City. Other interests include writing, YouTube, livestreams, web coding and nostalgia for early internet communities.

Personal preferences

Lewis's About page describes an appreciation for minimalism, clear night skies, stargazing, the Moon, nature, physical media, black-and-white television, discovering music, and the days of MSN Messenger. It also mentions an interest in space and the universe, amethyst, introspection and spending time at a laptop.

Dislikes listed on the same page include repetitive noises, large crowds, early mornings, UK heatwaves, TikTok, pineapple on pizza and the cost of living. These are presented as personal preferences rather than comprehensive biographical details.
