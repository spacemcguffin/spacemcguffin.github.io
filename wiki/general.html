List of Diary Entries (2021 – Present)

Each diary entry is presented as a television-style episode. Seasons 1 and 2 cover 2008–2009; Season 3 records the surviving 2012 entry and missing archive; Season 4 resumes in 2021.

The episode numbering continues across the archive, with Season 4 beginning at episode 2,138.

Episode numbering restarts at 1 for each quarterly part, while overall numbering continues across both seasons. Dates were reconstructed from the spreadsheet's inconsistent Excel date encoding.

About this page

This independent demonstration reproduces the general layout and table presentation of a Wikipedia-style episode list using replacement branding. It is not affiliated with Wikipedia, Wikimedia, Comedy Central or Paramount.

This is a fictional demonstration limited to seasons 1–5. Episode titles, credits, broadcast dates and production codes in the detailed tables are invented sample data, not historical records. The season overview uses real season counts and approximate historical release information for layout demonstration.
