Edge End High School is the main school in South Park. It is one of the oldest known locations on the show and has appeared in multiple incarnations.

Appearance

Edge End High School is a predominantly single-storey school, but with staffrooms and a library area on an upper floor.

IN RUINS

The first appearance of Edge End is in the pilot episode, where it has been destroyed and delapidated for multiple years. It remains in this state for the majority of Season 1.

REBUILT

During Season 2, the school is rebuilt, and has two predominant chimneys as a focal feature. To the rear is a giant playing field where the kids hang out during break times.

The rebuilt school becomes the standard version of Edge End seen throughout later episodes. Its exterior remains broadly consistent, although smaller background details, windows and surrounding features occasionally change between appearances.

Inside, the building contains a mixture of classrooms, corridors, offices and communal areas. Different parts of the school can be introduced whenever a story requires them, allowing the location to function as one of the show's main recurring settings.

The grounds surrounding Edge End are also used regularly as a meeting place for students. The playing field, entrances and paths around the building provide additional locations for scenes taking place before school, during breaks and at the end of the day.
